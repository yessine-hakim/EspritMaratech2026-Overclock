## Slide 1: THE PROBLEM (Hook)

### Imagine This...

**You're blind. You need groceries.**

You open an e-commerce app:
- ❌ Can't see product images
- ❌ Can't navigate complex menus
- ❌ Can't verify prices
- ❌ Can't complete checkout alone

**You're on a tight budget.**

The app recommends:
- ❌ Premium products you can't afford
- ❌ Items that blow your monthly budget
- ❌ No real-time affordability checks

### The Reality
- **1.3 billion people** with disabilities excluded from online shopping¹
- **60% of consumers** frustrated by unaffordable recommendations²
- **Digital divide** growing wider every day

## Slide 3: THE SOLUTION

### Voice-First Intelligent Shopping

**Complete shopping journey - hands-free, eyes-free**

🎙️ **"What is my balance?"**  
→ "You have 120 dinars available"

🎙️ **"Search for yogurt under 50 dinars"**  
→ Shows affordable options first

🎙️ **"Can I afford this cart?"**  
→ "Yes! Total is 85 dinars. You'll have 35 dinars remaining"

🎙️ **"Checkout"**
